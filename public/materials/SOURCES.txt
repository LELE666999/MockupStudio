Charcoal brick scene — added 2026-10-04:
Rough Concrete: https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_concrete
Original 1K diffuse, roughness, AO and displacement PNG; 2K OpenGL normal.
CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license
height.bin is a filtered 256 × 256 field derived from the original height PNG.
Locally authored closed brick geometry is displaced using that field; the
normal/roughness/AO maps add subpixel aggregate without baked lighting.
Refresh/checksum tool: scripts/download-brick-material.mjs.

Scene implementation references (no external demo JavaScript executed):
https://github.com/Erkaman/gl-rock — MIT; procedural chipped-rock design reference.
https://github.com/pmndrs/drei — MIT; existing installed RoundedBox/PivotControls.
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js — MIT; existing geometry and noise helpers.
Rock, slatted plinth and lattice geometry in this application are authored locally.
Travertine, slate, terrazzo and neutral fabric microstructure maps are authored
procedurally in utils/stageSurface.ts. Fur geometry and contact deformation are
authored locally. Linen uses the original CC0 normal and roughness maps; its
neutral albedo preserves the editable user-selected fabric color.
